Design decisions to push back on
Crowd picker: sheet, not inline
A sheet after the mood tap makes "who's listening" a single big-target choice and keeps the list uncluttered. The song list repeats it as an inline chip row so you can change it without going back. Push back if you'd rather skip the sheet entirely.
Style from your references
Warm near-black stage and beige paper (ref 3), glass cards, mono spec labels, white-pill segmented control and orange Play pill (ref 1), pastel dots for mood colours (ref 2). Barlow stands in for the DIN-like face in ref 3; SF Pro would also work.
One chord colour, two values
Chords are amber #FFA552 on stage (10:1) and burnt orange #B4430A on light (5:1). Same hue so they read as one system.
Play mode = whole screen is the button
Tap anywhere pauses; swipes for speed and exit. The only visible control is a 30pt ✕ inside a 44pt hit area. The current line gets a soft band; past lines fade; next lines stay at AAA contrast.
Icons are stand-ins
Material Symbols Rounded fills in for SF Symbols in these mockups (e.g. flame, heart, temple, radio). Swap to the SF equivalents in code.
Lyrics
Only the two sample songs' lyrics appear. Wonderwall's sheet reuses the Campfire Night sample lines with capo-2 shapes, to show long chord names (A7sus4, Cadd9) hanging over words. Real-song rows show starting chords instead of a first line.
